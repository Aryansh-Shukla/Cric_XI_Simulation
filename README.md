# 🏏 Cricket XI

**Draft Legends. Build Dynasties.**

Cricket XI is a strategic cricket drafting and simulation game where players build their ultimate all-time XI by selecting legendary cricketers from historical teams and tournaments.

Inspired by the fast-paced drafting mechanics that make games like 7a0 addictive, Cricket XI creates a completely original cricket experience focused on squad building, historical eras, team balance, and tournament simulation.

Every run is different.

Every draft matters.

Every selection can change history.

---

## 🎮 What is Cricket XI?

Cricket XI challenges players to build the strongest possible cricket team by drafting players one round at a time from historical squads.

You may begin with:

- India 2011
- Australia 2003
- Pakistan 1992
- England 2019
- CSK 2023
- MI 2020

and gradually assemble a dream XI featuring legends from different eras.

After drafting, your team enters a full tournament simulation where team composition, player traits, leadership, chemistry, match conditions, and tournament progression determine whether your dynasty becomes immortal.

A complete run takes approximately **5–8 minutes**.

## ✨ Features

### 🏆 Historical Drafting

Draft players from iconic squads across cricket history.

Examples:

- India 2011
- Australia 1999
- Australia 2003
- Pakistan 1992
- England 2019
- IPL franchise seasons

Every draft presents unique decisions.

---

### 🧠 Build Your Dream XI

Construct a balanced team while satisfying cricket-specific requirements:

- At least 1 wicketkeeper
- Minimum 5 bowling options
- At least 2 pace bowlers
- At least 1 spinner
- Maximum 7 specialist batsmen
- IPL overseas player restrictions

No two runs are identical.

---

### 🌍 Tournament Modes

Compete in:

- ODI World Cup
- T20 World Cup
- Champions Trophy
- IPL
- Test Cricket

Each tournament follows its own qualification and knockout structure.

## 🏗 Architecture

Cricket XI is designed with a scalable architecture that can support future online features and database integration.

### Current Architecture

```text
Data
↓
Repositories
↓
Services
↓
Simulation Engine
↓
UI
```

This separation allows future migration to Supabase or other backend systems without rewriting core gameplay logic.
